One total. Every cost included.

Pricing method and scenario assumptions
  • Valuation date: 17 November 2026. USD is the calculation currency; UZS = USD × 12,023.61. A different exchange rate requires rebuilding the local/imported cost split; this is not an FX forecast.
  • A = source-anchored installed allowance; E = estimator assumption without a current matching quotation. No installed line is a firm sourced tender. High confidence applies to arithmetic/CBU rate; Medium to several anchors; most unmeasured quantities and specialist allowances are Low.
  • Direct rates exclude VAT and include trade margin, delivery, labour and waste unless separated. Where a source does not state VAT, its anchor is provisionally treated as VAT-inclusive and divided by 1.12. Reconfirm this before tendering.
  • Low / expected / high: management 4 / 5 / 6%; design 2.5 / 3.5 / 5%; contingency 10 / 15 / 20%; escalation 0 / 4 / 8%. Bases are stated in F. Contingency covers design/scope development beyond listed quantities; rate ranges cover procurement variability. These are not P10/P50/P90 estimates.
  • Finance: 40 / 60 / 70% debt; 18 / 24 / 30% annual interest; 12 / 15 / 18 months; average drawdown 50%. These are assumptions, not offers. Expected borrowing is local-currency debt expressed at today's FX.
  • Standard VAT 12% is applied once to modeled taxable works/services and associated risk/escalation reserves. Land, statutory permits, insurance, holding tax and interest are excluded from that base. No input-tax recovery, exemption, subsidy or electricity export income is credited.

03 / Value of sustainability

Green premium, without double counting

Expected-cost comparison with the same size, seismic structure, safe basic services and economy finish. The baseline is a cost comparator, not a claim that lower specifications meet local requirements. Direct premiums are net of VAT and already included in A–E; do not add them again. Programme management, contingency, VAT and finance are excluded here.

Solar PV: check who can use the power

Water reuse: cost it as resilience

Assume 350 mm/year rainfall for the class calculation, not a verified weather series. Collection: 600 m² unplanted roof × 0.8 runoff × 0.35 m = 168 m³/year theoretical. Use 85 m³/year after timing/storage losses, pending a monthly water balance.

The latest local primary tariff located is 2,800 × 1.12 = 3,136 UZS/m³. Thus 85 m³ saves only about USD 22/year before maintenance. [WATER] Rainwater O&M is assumed USD 150/year; greywater O&M USD 600/year. Neither has a positive direct utility payback.

Greywater is capped at 250 m³/year of landscape demand. Combined reuse is 335 m³/year versus assumed demand 900 m² × 0.4 m³/m²/year = 360 m³. Do not apply a blanket 35% saving or count the same avoided water twice. No sewer-charge saving is credited for irrigation.

Lifecycle assumptions and limits

Energy yields, self-consumption, heat losses, life and maintenance are estimator assumptions. A PV shading study, hourly load profile, thermal model and monthly water balance are needed.

  • 20-year PV illustration: 5% real discount rate, 0.5% annual degradation, constant real tariffs, 1.5% annual O&M on net installed capex, and USD 2,000 + VAT inverter replacement in year 12. No export income, batteries, subsidy, financing, salvage or carbon credits.
  • Passive measures interact. Insulation, glazing, shading, mass and ventilation savings are assessed as one package, not summed as independent percentages.
  • Passive package sensitivity: 70–110 MWh/year useful heating avoided plus 6–12 MWh/year cooling electricity avoided. Gas conversion assumes 9.5 kWh/m³ and 85% efficiency. At 1,100–2,000 UZS/m³ gas and 1,100 UZS/kWh electricity, gross savings are about USD 1,340–3,365/year. These are assumptions, not simulated results.
  • Solar hot water: 20 m² × 500 kWh/m²/year = 10,000 useful kWh/year. The same gas conversion at 2,000 UZS/m³ gives about USD 206/year avoided fuel. Less USD 96/year O&M gives USD 110/year; USD 7,168 VAT-inclusive capex implies roughly 65 years simple payback. Lower domestic gas tariffs weaken it further.
  • Confirm meter/tariff classification and who receives the savings. The owner may pay for equipment while residents receive the benefit. No unverified incentives are included.
04 / Design to budget

Keep the benefits. Test the trade-offs.

Options recalculate the expected scenario, including fees, contingency, VAT and finance. They are not added to the low-case discount. Contributed land and equity reduce cash requirements; land and capital still have economic value.

Additional savings to develop, not bank yet

  • Repeat layouts and stack wet rooms: test 5–8% reduction in C07–C09 and C12 through shorter runs and repeated details. Not deducted until measured.
  • Standardize windows, doors and kitchens: obtain three comparable local quotes; seek 5–10% package savings without reducing safety or durability.
  • Optimize the structural grid after geotechnical investigation: quantify concrete/rebar with the engineer. Do not cut seismic reinforcement, fire safety, egress or accessibility.
  • Prioritize passive design: retain orientation, insulation, operable windows, targeted shading, shade trees and efficient lighting. Use certified local AAC and repairable finishes.
  • Match PV to the load: retain 30 kW if an approved metering arrangement can consume its output; phased 15 kW may be better if common loads are small. No speculative export income.
  • Local/recycled materials: use tested reclaimed pavers/timber where suitable; no unsupported discount or uncertified structural substitution.

Disproportionate cost flags: full greywater reuse, rainwater-to-toilet plumbing, and solar thermal at low gas tariffs. Rooftop planting is already removed. They may provide resilience or teaching value, but direct bill savings do not justify them in the expected case. A decorative fountain is excluded.

05 / Team decisions

Assumptions to verify before the next estimate

  1. Areas: 10 × 40 + 20 × 60 + 10 × 80 = 2,400 m² net; 500 m² gross common/services; net-to-gross 82.8%. 600 m² × 5 = 3,000 m², so 2,900 m² assumes setbacks/voids averaging 580 m²/floor. Confirm balcony/GFA measurement.
  2. Model coordination: this plan follows your 5-storey, 40-unit, 2,500 m² brief. The viewer's 4,176 m² plot and older 4-storey/48-unit Python/JSON model are not quantity take-off sources.
  3. Soil/seismic: intensity 8 is a brief assumption. Confirm microzonation, bearing, settlement, groundwater and foundation scheme. Shallow foundations, no basement, piles, retaining walls or major dewatering assumed. The range does not cover unlimited ground risk.
  4. Structure: 360 + 320 + 580 = 1,260 m³ concrete before ground slab/stairs/balconies. Corresponding steel is 187.6 t (~64.7 kg/m² GFA). Verify spans, shear walls, ductility, joints and material grades.
  5. One elevator: assume one accessible 630 kg, 1 m/s, machine-room-less passenger lift serving five stops. B29 covers incremental shaft/pit; C26–28 cover equipment, installation and power. Confirm cabin clearances, lobby access from all eight homes per floor, pit/headroom, rescue, fire strategy and local certification. Keep 500 m² gross common/services only if the revised core fits; otherwise increase GFA or reduce net area. Allow USD 600–1,200/year service plus 1,500–3,000 kWh/year electricity as unquoted operating assumptions, outside capex.
  6. Land/services: verify title/development rights, lawful acquisition, valuation, utility capacity and distances. No demolition, relocation or major off-site network/road extension.
  7. Envelope: verify 350 m² glazing, 1,300 m² opaque facade, 300 mm AAC, acoustic/fire separation, certified 100 mm facade insulation and 150 mm roof insulation. RC floors provide mass; fired-brick substitution requires recosting and load checks.
  8. MEP: confirm gas, 200 kW boiler sizing, heat loss, freeze protection, DHW, fire tanks/pumps/sprinklers. One lift is included; apartment AC is excluded.
  9. Roof: coordinate the 600 m² unplanted roof with PV setbacks, routes, lift overrun and plant. Roof soil/vegetation is excluded. Retain insulation, drainage, wind uplift and seismic anchoring checks.
  10. Water: confirm reuse approvals, backflow safeguards, treatment, seasonal irrigation, testing and maintenance. Yields are provisional; no guaranteed 35% saving.
  11. Finishes: one kitchen/bathroom per home, 3/4/5 internal doors. No appliances, furniture, premium stone, decorative ceilings, fountain or basement parking.
  12. Commercial: confirm start date, 12–18 month programme, supplier VAT, FX exposure, finance, tax recovery and target scope. Retain 15% expected contingency until drawings, soil data and quotes reduce uncertainty.
  13. Lifecycle: assign maintenance of the elevator, PV, filters, pumps and ground-level planting. Include residents' service charges in the affordability discussion.
