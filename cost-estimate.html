Context — Is USD 550/m² realistic?
Industry data shows basic/affordable construction in Uzbekistan costs USD 275–510/m² (hard costs only, economy finish), while standard specification is USD 380–710/m² (Source: enormousbuildings.com, 2024 index). Your target of USD 550/m² including green features, soft costs, and seismic engineering is ambitious but not unreasonable for a regional city if green features are kept proportionate. A more realistic "green affordable" benchmark is USD 600–700/m² all-in.

✂️ 4. Cost-Saving Options

Options to bring the project closer to the USD 550/m² target without losing key sustainability benefits.

Option Savings (USD) Trade-off Recommendation
1. Reduce green roof to 100 m² (half area) 5,400 Less stormwater retention, less cooling. Keep area near solar panels for dual benefit. ✅ Recommended
2. Phase solar PV: install 15 kW now, add 15 kW later 12,000 Half the electricity offset initially. Pre-wire for expansion. Residents fund Phase 2 from savings. ✅ Recommended
3. Use AAC blocks instead of fired brick for infill 8,000 AAC is lighter (less seismic mass), self-insulating. May reduce insulation thickness to 80mm. Locally produced in UZ. ✅ Strongly recommended
4. Defer greywater system to Phase 2 8,000 Lose 35% irrigation water savings initially. Pre-plumb grey drain separation now ($1,000 extra) for future retrofit. ✅ Recommended
5. Simplify sun shading: use deep window recesses (300mm) + fixed concrete overhangs instead of aluminum louvers 8,000 Concrete overhangs are integral to structure (lower marginal cost). Less adjustability but ~70% as effective. ✅ Recommended
6. Basic interior: handover at "rough finish" (chernovoy) 100,000+ Residents finish their own interiors (very common in UZ affordable housing). Dramatically cuts D category. ⚡ High savings but reduces livability at handover
7. Reduce balcony railings to steel only (no glass) 9,000 Steel bar railings instead of glass infill. Still safe, less aesthetic. ✅ Recommended for economy
8. Self-perform landscaping with community labor 8,000 Community planting days, residents help with landscaping. Builds ownership. ✅ Recommended — aligns with circular economy
9. Negotiate bulk material pricing (cement, rebar, brick) 15,000–25,000 UZEX commodity exchange bulk pricing. 5–10% below retail is achievable. ✅ Always do this
10. Apply for Solnechny Dom (Solar Home) subsidy (20%) 4,900 Government covers 20% of solar PV cost for eligible projects. ✅ Apply — it's free money
